Final season tables -- Gold Coast 1976 to 2025 -- Index

The Gold Coast and District Soccer Association was founded in late 1975 with seven teams from five clubs competing in its initial senior season.

By 1985 the association had attracted clubs from north of the Gold Coast and was renamed the Gold Coast and Albert Soccer Federation to reflect this.

In 1987 the association absorbed the Tweed District Soccer Association incorporating clubs from Northern NSW into its competitions.

The top division became the Gold Coast Premier League from 1991 to 2021, and the association was known as Gold Coast Soccer from 1996 to 2011, after which it became Football Gold Coast.

A restructure of Queensland football saw the Gold Coast league renamed as Football Queensland South Coast in late 2021.

Several Gold Coast teams competed in Brisbane competition prior to the formation of the NPL in 2013: Gold Coast United (1966-1981), Coolangatta (1969-1971), Nerang (1972-1996), Twin Towns (1975-1982) and Palm Beach (1998-2007).

Since 2013 the following Gold Coast based teams have competed in the NPL, FQPL1 and FQPL2: Palm Beach, Gold Coast United, Gold Coast Knights, Coomera, Surfers Paradise Apollo, Magic United, Broadbeach United, Robina City.
