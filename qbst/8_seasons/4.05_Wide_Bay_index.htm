Final season tables -- Wide Bay 2013 to 2025 -- Index

Local regional associations in Bundaberg, Hervey Bay, Maryborough and Gympie conducted their own premier competitions for senior men until 2012.

Football Wide Bay was founded in 2005 but only commenced a premier senior men's region-wide league in 2013.

A restructure of Queensland football saw Football Wide Bay rebadged as Football Queensland Wide Bay in late 2021.

Football Gympie clubs had historically competed in both Wide Bay and Sunshine Coast competitions, but moved across completely to Sunshine Coast Football from the 2019 season when Football Gympie was dissolved.

 

A ranking of clubs by grand final and league premiership wins is listed below.

 

Also listed below are seasons for Bundaberg (2004 to 2012) and Hervey Bay (2003 to 2012).
