Final season tables -- Macarthur FA 2001 to 2025 -- Index

The list below sets out the premiership winners and Grand Final winners (where known) for every top flight football in the Macarthur league since 2001.

The association was founded in 1983, originally as the Macarthur District Soccer Football Association, then renamed the Macarthur Football Association in 2013.

The M League commenced in 2009, since which a final series has been played (except 2021).

Information on seasons prior to 2000 is currently unavailable.

 

A ranking of clubs by premiership wins is listed below.
